# Overview

**Overview** is where Bardo opens: the open project at a glance. It lists the project's videos in progress with the step that moves each one on, the ideas waiting for you, and beside them the project's channels, its spending this month and its narrators. [Show me](bardo:tour/overview).

<a id="navigation"></a>
## Finding your way

The navigation (the sidebar in the Workspace layout, the top tabs in Studio) has two sections:

- **Project**: Overview, Ideas ([Research](niche-research.md) and [Themes](themes-ranking.md) under two tabs), [Videos](projects.md), [Narrators](personas.md), [Products](products.md), [Templates](templates.md), Channels and accounts ([Channels](channels.md) and [Accounts](network-accounts.md) under two tabs) and [Performance](performance-metrics.md).
- **Global library**: [the narrators, products and templates every project can use](global-library.md).

Jobs, Costs, the Guide and Settings stay pinned at the foot of the sidebar, or at the end of the top tabs.

<a id="videos"></a>
## Videos in progress

**Pick up where you left off** shows one card for each video of the project that is not posted yet, newest first. Each card has the video's title, its channel and format (an ad says so), the stage of its next step and how that stage stands, as [Videos](projects.md) shows it ("Clips: 7 of 14 clips").

A video already posted on a network is left out. A video whose post waits to go out is still listed, and says it waits.

<a id="next-steps"></a>
## The next step

Each card says what moves the video on, in the accent color: confirm a brief, write or review the script, record the narration, plan the scenes, draw or review images, animate clips, open the editor to build the cut, render, export, and finally upload it or link its post. Bardo picks the first stage, in order, that waits on you.

When the step calls a paid provider, the card shows what it would cost ("about $1.40"), from the same estimate the stage shows before it starts. **Continue** opens the video at that stage; building the cut opens the [editor](editor.md).

Some videos wait for nothing you can do:

- while a job of the video runs, the card says Bardo is working on that stage;
- once the post waits to go out, scheduled in Bardo or on its network, the card says when, and **Open** shows the video.

<a id="ideas"></a>
## Ideas waiting for you

**Ideas waiting for you** counts the project's themes still to rank, the ranked ones waiting to be approved or discarded, and approved themes whose video is gone. **Open Ideas** goes to [Themes](themes-ranking.md).

<a id="project"></a>
## The project

Beside the videos, Overview lists the project's channels with their language and country, what the project's work spent this month (the same amount [Costs](costs.md#projects) shows for the project), and the narrators its channels use by default.
