# Missed posts

Instagram takes no publish time from apps, so a Reel scheduled in Bardo is posted by Bardo itself at its time ([Scheduling](uploading.md#schedule)). When Bardo is closed or the computer is off at that time, nothing is posted, and nothing is posted later without you either. The next time Bardo opens, the list **Scheduled posts that didn't go out** comes up over the window for you to decide on each one. [Show me the list](bardo:tour/missed).

<a id="why"></a>
## Why a post is missed

A post is missed when its time passes without it: Bardo was closed or the computer was off, or its upload failed and couldn't go on. Each post in the list shows its title, network, account and when it was due. While the list is up, the rest of the window waits, and so do the guided tours, except the list's own (**Tour this list**).

YouTube videos are never missed: YouTube publishes them by itself. TikTok drafts are never scheduled by Bardo.

<a id="send"></a>
## Post now

**Post now** posts it right away, as it was reviewed.

<a id="new-time"></a>
## New time

**New time** asks for a later date and time, read in your computer's time zone, and **Save time** keeps it. Keep Bardo open at that time, or the post is missed again.

<a id="cancel"></a>
## Cancel post

**Cancel post** asks first, then drops the post: nothing goes to the network. To post it later, review the upload again at the project's [Publish](bardo:go/projects/publish) stage.

<a id="later"></a>
## Decide later

**Decide later** closes the list for this session. Each post keeps waiting at its project's Publish stage, with the same three choices, and the list comes back the next time Bardo opens. Opening the guide from the list's tour (**Learn more** or F1) closes the list the same way.
