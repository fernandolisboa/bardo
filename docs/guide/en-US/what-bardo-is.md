# What Bardo is

Bardo helps you run faceless channels on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels, X and Kick: it finds what to make, produces the video with AI, lets you edit it by hand, and publishes it. AI does the heavy lifting; you decide every step.

<a id="pillars"></a>
## The pillars

Everything in Bardo belongs to one of four pillars, in this order:

- **Strategy**: choose a niche and a video idea. [Research](bardo:go/research) scores niches by competition and trend, [Themes](bardo:go/themes) suggests and ranks video ideas, and [Performance](bardo:go/performance) shows how your published videos did.
- **Production**: make the video's material. A [project](bardo:go/projects) goes from the script to the narration, the scene images and the clips. [Personas](bardo:go/personas) are your narrators, and [Templates](bardo:go/templates) hold the instructions the AI writes from.
- **Editing**: the editor, where you cut the picture, mix the audio, place the captions and frame the video for each network, then render it.
- **Publishing**: your [channels](bardo:go/channels) and their [accounts](bardo:go/accounts) on each network, the text of each post, uploads, schedules and exports.

The navigation groups the screens by pillar. Jobs, Costs, Guide and Settings are always at hand, at the foot of the sidebar or on the right of the top bar.

<a id="flow"></a>
## From a niche to a published video

1. Create a channel: its niche, language, target country and narrator.
2. Research niches and pick the one with room to grow.
3. Ask for video ideas, keep the best and approve one. Approving starts a video project.
4. Generate and review the script, then the narration, the scenes and, if you want, short clips.
5. Open the editor, shape the cut, then review and render it for each network.
6. Upload it, schedule it, or export a ready-to-post package.
7. Follow how it does, and let that shape your next ideas.

[Your first video](first-video.md) walks through each step.

<a id="control"></a>
## You stay in control

- **AI suggests, you decide.** Every script, prompt, image and post text can be edited before it is used, and a new version waits for your review next to the current one.
- **Nothing irreversible happens on its own.** A render or an upload starts only after a review screen and your confirmation.
- **Your own accounts.** Bardo calls each AI provider with your own key and posts through apps you register, so you see and control what is spent. See [Your API keys](api-keys.md).
- **Your files stay on your computer.** Projects live in your user folder, and keys and sign-ins are kept in Windows Credential Manager.

<a id="around"></a>
## Finding your way around

- **Layouts**: Workspace (a sidebar by pillar, cards with details beside them) or Studio (top tabs, denser tables). Choose one in [Settings › Appearance](bardo:go/settings/appearance); every screen does the same things in both.
- **Jobs**: long work, like a narration, images or a render, runs as a job. Keep working meanwhile, and follow, cancel or resume each job from Jobs.
- **Guide**: the [welcome tour](bardo:tour/welcome), this user guide (F1 from any screen but the editor) and the [keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md).
